1. **Always warm, never cold** - Use cream/beige backgrounds, not pure white
2. **Natural materials** - Reference bamboo, wood, ceramic in design
3. **Traditional but not outdated** - Refined rustic, not overly vintage
4. **Readable Vietnamese text** - Ensure diacritics display properly
5. **Food is the hero** - Photography should be prominent and appetizing
6. **Atmospheric** - Warm shadows, intimate lighting effects
7. **Accessible** - Maintain WCAG AA contrast ratios

## 💡 Common Questions

### Q: Can I use pure white backgrounds?

**A:** Prefer warm cream (`#F5EBD9`) instead. Pure white feels too clinical for the warm, traditional aesthetic.

### Q: What font should I use?

**A:**

- **Headings:** Playfair Display or Cormorant (serif)
- **Body:** Inter or Nunito Sans (sans-serif)
- **Must support:** Vietnamese diacritics

### Q: How do I handle mobile responsive design?

**A:**

- Mobile: 1 column, touch-friendly
- Tablet: 2 columns
- Desktop: 3 columns
- Always food images prominent, easy menu access

### Q: Can I add dark mode?

**A:** Yes, but maintain warmth. Use dark brown (`#2C1810`) instead of pure black. Keep the red accent and ensure food images remain appetizing.

### Q: What about animations?

**A:** Keep them subtle and smooth (300ms transitions). Gentle scale on hover (1.05x), warm glow shadows, fade-ins. Never fast or jarring.

## 🚨 Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Don't:**

- Use pure white (`#FFFFFF`) for main backgrounds
- Use cool blues except ceramic accents
- Make animations fast or jarring
- Forget Vietnamese diacritics support
- Use generic "Asian" aesthetics
- Make design too modern/minimalist

✅ **Do:**

- Use warm cream (`#F5EBD9`) for backgrounds
- Reference natural materials (bamboo, wood)
- Keep animations smooth (300ms)
- Test Vietnamese text rendering
- Stay specific to Hue/Central Vietnam
- Balance traditional and contemporary
